Screens in the builder: several questions on one slides screen.

The layout copy now says what slides do with screens, in the create-form
dialog and in Design: "One screen at a time. Group questions to show several
on one screen." (`forms.layoutSlidesDesc`, `admin.editor.layout.slidesHint`).
A new `admin.editor.layout.screensIgnored` note sits under the layout picker on
One page when the form has screens: they only apply to Slides, and they are
kept, so switching back restores them. Both locales.
