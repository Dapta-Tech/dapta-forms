HighLevel can be connected and used as a destination from the dashboard.

- Integrations page: a HighLevel card asks for the Private Integration token and the Location ID (or the sub-account's URL), with the three setup steps in HighLevel's own menu labels, the three scopes as its scope search lists them, and where to turn Private Integrations on (Settings > Labs). Each refusal reason from the API has its own sentence (`token_format`, `invalid_token`, `wrong_location`, `missing_scope`, `unreachable` with its cause, plus a timeout, a role that may not connect and a server without an encryption key). Connected, it says "Connected to {sub-account}", the Location ID, the token's last four and the date, with Replace token (same call, every form keeps its mapping) and Disconnect. When HighLevel stops accepting the token the badge and an alert say so. Members see it all read-only.
- A form's Connect tab: a HighLevel card with the mapping in a Standard group and a Custom fields group, Refresh fields, only the custom fields whose type can take the answer, the score to a number field, the submitted date to a date field and each UTM to a text field. Switching it on for the first time proposes email, phone, name and company, and any `utm_*` custom fields the sub-account has. It warns when neither Email nor Phone is mapped, says HighLevel decides duplicates by the sub-account's Contact Duplication Preferences (and that phone matching can merge people who share a number), takes tags as chips (lowercase, added and never removed), a source (default "dForms: {form}"), a day time zone, fixed values (never on Email or Phone), the "Send partial responses too" switch (off by default; `events` is written only once it is touched) and the delivery history. Not connected, it connects in place and links to Integrations. One per form; a stored config round-trips exactly.
- The builder's Connect panel loads the HighLevel connection and fields like Salesforce's.
- The delivery history translates HighLevel's reasons. English and Spanish.
